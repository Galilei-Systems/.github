# Galilei Systems

Galilei Systems is a software company founded in 2026 in West Palm Beach, Florida. We build operating platforms: software that brings into a single system what a business today spreads across many tools.

## Galilei Hub

Our first product is [Galilei Hub](https://galileihub.com/en/), the real estate CRM and operating platform for Florida brokerages. CRM, commissions, documents, e-signature, communications and automations in a single operation, with a dedicated portal for every role in the transaction: broker, agent, lender, title attorney, transaction coordinator and property manager.

Natively bilingual in English and Spanish. Available on web, iPhone, Apple Watch and CarPlay.
